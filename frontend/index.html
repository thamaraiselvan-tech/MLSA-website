The Program

What is MLSA?

The Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors (MLSA) program is a global community of student leaders passionate about technology. Ambassadors get early access to Microsoft tools and resources, mentorship opportunities, and the chance to lead workshops, hackathons, and study groups on their own campus — earning recognition through tiered badges like Associate along the way.
