About

The Chapter

We are the Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors of Saranathan College of Engineering (Dept. of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science) — a student-driven tech community committed to making cutting-edge technology accessible to everyone on campus. Our chapter organizes hands-on Azure cloud workshops, Microsoft certification bootcamps, and flagship hackathons like AI StartUp Arena. Through peer-to-peer mentorship and practical project building, we empower students across departments to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world industry skills.

The Global Program

What is MLSA?

The Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors (MLSA) program is a global network of passionate student leaders dedicated to technology, community building, and peer learning. Ambassadors receive early access to Microsoft developer tools, Azure cloud credits, and mentorship from industry MVPs to host campus workshops and study circles. Designed to foster both technical expertise and leadership qualities, MLSA serves as a launchpad for students to build real-world software, earn industry certifications, and connect with a global community of innovators.
