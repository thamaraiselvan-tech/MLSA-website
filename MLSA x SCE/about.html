Our Purpose

Democratizing Tech Skills on Campus

The MLSA × SCE Chapter was founded with a clear goal: to make cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and professional software development tools accessible to every Saranathan engineering student, regardless of prior experience.

Equipping students with industry-ready capabilities beyond standard textbook curricula through practical building and peer mentorship.

What started as a small cohort of student builders has grown into an active campus hub organizing structured study paths, Azure deployment labs, certification prep, and rapid startup hackathons.
