About MLSA × SCE Chapter

Empowering campus innovation through Microsoft technology, peer mentorship, hands-on workshops, and real-world developer experiences.

Our Purpose

Chapter Mission & Vision

The MLSA × SCE Chapter was established within the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Saranathan College of Engineering with a single core goal: to democratize cutting-edge tech skills and make cloud computing, AI, and developer tools accessible to every student on campus, regardless of prior experience.

What started as a small cohort of passionate student builders has evolved into a thriving campus community. We organize structured learning paths, practical deployment sessions, Microsoft certification prep, and high-energy hackathons — equipping peers with industry-ready capabilities beyond standard textbook curricula.

The Worldwide Initiative

What is the Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors Program?

The global MLSA program brings together tech-passionate students from universities across the globe to learn, lead, and share knowledge.

⚡

Early Technology Access

Ambassadors receive access to Microsoft Azure cloud credits, Visual Studio Enterprise tools, Microsoft Copilot APIs, and exclusive developer preview resources to experiment and build campus solutions.

🌐

Peer Mentorship & Events

Ambassadors lead workshops, hackathons, and study groups on their own campus — gaining public speaking, leadership, and community management experience alongside global peers.

🏆

Milestone Recognition

Students advance through tiered ambassador milestones — New, Alpha, Beta, and Gold — unlocking mentorship from Microsoft MVPs, event sponsorships, and career guidance.

Campus Pillars

Our Key Initiatives at Saranathan

How our chapter drives continuous skill development for engineering students.

🎓

Hands-on Technical Workshops

Interactive coding bootcamps covering web development, cloud deployment on Vercel/Azure, AI prompt engineering, and GitHub workflows handled by student ambassadors.

📜

Certification & Azure Readiness

Guiding students through claiming free Azure for Students credits ($100), understanding cloud fundamentals, and preparing for official Microsoft certification exams.

🚀

Hackathons & Pitch Competitions

Flagship events like AI StartUp Arena where student teams ideate, prototype, and pitch AI startup concepts using Microsoft Copilot and Designer in 60 minutes.

🤝

Open Community & Mentorship

An active WhatsApp community and peer network connecting junior students with senior mentors to solve project roadblocks, share resources, and collaborate.

Department & Faculty Collaboration

The MLSA × SCE Chapter operates with institutional backing from the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Saranathan College of Engineering, under the guidance of Dr. S. Ravimaran (Head of the Department). This collaboration ensures student initiatives receive guidance, venue support, and academic visibility across campus.
